How to remove your Bubbles account and data

You can delete your Bubbles account and everything associated with it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

Option 1 — In the app (instant)

1
Open Bubbles and go to Settings.
2
Tap Your account.
3
Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and trial record are removed immediately.

Option 2 — By email

If you no longer have the app installed, email makarbsaleh@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up, with the subject "Delete my Bubbles account".

Requests are actioned within 30 days, and normally within a few days. You will get a confirmation once it is done. No account is needed to make this request.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Your email addressDeleted permanently
Your account identifierDeleted permanently
Your free-trial recordDeleted permanently

Nothing is retained after deletion, and there is no backup copy kept beyond the routine backup rotation of the underlying service, which expires within 30 days.

Two things worth knowing first

Your starters and baking history are not affected. They live on your phone, not on a server, so deleting your account leaves them completely intact. To remove those as well, use Settings → Reset all data in the app, or uninstall it.

Your Bubbles Pro purchase is not affected. It belongs to your Google Play account, not your Bubbles account, and can be restored at any time with Settings → Restore purchase.
