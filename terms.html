Bubbles offers general guidance about sourdough starters and baking. It is informational only and is not a food safety authority.

Always use your own judgement. Discard any starter or dough that shows fuzzy or coloured mould, smells strongly of acetone, solvent or rot, or has changed colour in a way you cannot explain. When in doubt, throw it out and begin again — a starter costs flour and a week, which is a much better trade than getting ill.

Bubbles cannot inspect your starter and cannot tell you that something is safe to eat. Baking and consuming what you make is at your own risk.
